## Generated speech

MP3 word audio is synthesized locally using Piper and the en_US-ljspeech-medium voice. It is synthetic speech, not a human dictionary recording. The voice model card identifies the training dataset as public domain:

- https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_US/ljspeech/medium
- https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/

The Piper engine, ONNX model and build dependencies are not distributed inside this application or repository. Build tools remain in ignored `scripts/work`; only generated MP3 files are packaged. The voice is not presented as an endorsement by its dataset contributors.
